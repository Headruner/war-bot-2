Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

This page describes what EVE Distance Meter and its Discord bot ("the service") handle. It is run by the person who hosts this instance; the data stays on that person's server.

What is collected

When you sign in with EVE Online: your character ID and name, and the access and refresh tokens that EVE SSO gives for the permissions (scopes) you approve. Tokens are stored encrypted. With those permissions the service reads, for your characters only, things such as location, ship, online status, notifications, structures and assets, and searches mail for the names of structures. This data is used to rank your characters and to show structure information; mail is read only for that purpose.

In the browser: a session cookie that keeps you signed in and a few display preferences. The web server may keep ordinary access logs (such as IP address and time).

Discord: the bot posts messages into the channels that the operator configured. It does not read messages or collect information about Discord users. It stores only the IDs of the servers and channels it is set up for.

Other services

To work, the service calls EVE Online's ESI, zKillboard and Discord. Their own policies apply to the data they receive. Nothing is sold or used for advertising.

How long

Data is kept while the character is linked. Alerts and notifications are removed automatically after a set time (by default 7 and 30 days). Removing a character on the Characters page deletes its tokens and revokes them at EVE. You can also revoke access yourself at CCP's third-party applications page, and remove the bot from your Discord server at any time.

Your choices

You decide which permissions to approve, and you can remove your characters or revoke access whenever you like. To ask for your data to be deleted, contact the person who runs this instance. They will do what they reasonably can, but make no commitment about timing or outcome.

No guarantee

Reasonable care is taken with stored data, but no system is completely secure and no guarantee is given. The operator accepts no liability for loss or misuse of data beyond what the law requires.

Changes

This policy may change at any time by updating this page.
