Source: "with assets" is the default list. "Can dock, no assets" adds structures that ESI's character search (esi-search.search_structures.v1) says some character may dock at, even without items there; the page shows how many characters can dock, never which. "Named in mails" lists structures that appear as a link in an in-game mail of a character that granted esi-mail.read_mail.v1; only the structure, its owner corporation and the mail's date are kept, never the mail itself.
